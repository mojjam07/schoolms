# Guide for administrators

Menu names below are the ones in the left-hand menu. If a name is missing from your menu, ask your developer to add it.

## First-time set-up (do these in this order, once)
1. **Sessions and terms**: create the school year (for example 2026/2027) with its three terms and their dates. Mark the current session and term.
2. **Classes and subjects**: create the classes (JSS 1A, JSS 1B…) and the subjects, then say which subjects each class takes.
3. **Grading and assessment**: confirm the grade scale and the score columns (for example CA 1, CA 2, Exam) with the school's leadership. Change nothing after scores are entered without asking your developer.
4. **Fee setup**: enter the fees for each class and term, and any standing discounts.
5. **Accounts**: create the teachers, students and parents. Each person gets an activation link to set their own password. For many students at once use **Import students** (upload a CSV, run the **dry run** first, fix any lines it lists, then import).
6. **Students and classes**: put each student in a class for the session.
7. **Teaching assignments**: say which teacher teaches which subject in which class. Teachers see only what they are assigned.
8. **Form teachers**: choose one form teacher per class. They take the daily register.
9. **Guardians**: link each parent to their children. **Only you can create these links**, because they decide who can see a child's results and fees. Mark one parent as the primary contact.
10. **Timetable** and **Exam schedules**: build them, then publish. Students, parents and teachers are notified.

## Every day and every week
- **Overview** (the home page) shows people counts, students by class, assignments waiting to be marked, recent applications, tuition collected and outstanding, announcements and the latest administrator actions. A "Needs attention" box lists what needs a decision.
- **Attendance**: see which classes have taken today's register. You can take or correct a register for any class and date, and review absence explanations.
- **Messages** is the website enquiries box. **Office messages** is where parents write to the school office: reply from there. **All conversations** lets you read any parent and staff conversation for safeguarding, and remove a message that breaks the rules. Opening a conversation is recorded.

## Fees
- **Invoices and payments**: open an invoice to record a payment received at the office (cash, transfer or POS), add a discount or extra charge (with a reason), set the pay-by date, remind the parent, or reverse a payment (with a reason). Payments are never edited: a mistake is reversed, which keeps the record.
- **Instalment plan**: on an invoice choose **Set up an instalment plan** (2 to 6 instalments). The parts must add up exactly to the invoice. Payments are matched to the earliest instalment first. If you add a charge or discount later, the plan shows "out of date": rebuild it.
- **Download statement (PDF)** and a **PDF** button on each payment give a printable statement and receipt. Parents can download their own.
- **Online payments** shows Paystack payments and anything that needs your attention.

## Results
Teachers enter scores. In **Results** you check them, publish a class's results for a term, and preview any student's report card. Parents and students can see results only after you publish. Publishing freezes the grading scale used.

## People
- **Accounts**: edit a name, phone, number or email; suspend or reactivate. Changing an email signs the person out; an unactivated account gets a fresh activation link.
- **Guardian requests**: parents ask for a link to be corrected (relationship, remove a child, add a child). **Check who is asking before you approve**, because approving changes who can see the child's records. You can add a note to your answer.

## New session
**New session promotion** previews, then runs once: moves students up, graduates the last year, handles repeaters and leavers, and carries over form teachers and teaching assignments. Take a backup first. Set up the new session's fees and grading columns by hand afterwards.

## Admissions and the public website
**Applications** lists applicants. For an applicant with an offer, **Make this applicant a student** creates the account (and optionally the guardian). **Website content**, **News and events** and **Facilities and gallery** edit the public site.

## Privacy and records
**Data and privacy** removes old records that the school no longer needs and exports everything held about one person when they ask. Check who is asking before sending an export. **Audit log** shows who did what and when; it cannot be edited.

## Rules to keep
- Never share your password. Sign out on shared computers.
- Give administrator accounts only to people who really run the school.
- Keep fee and result figures out of group chats.
