# Guide for parents and guardians

## Signing in
The school sends you an email with an activation link. Open it and choose your password. After that, sign in with your email and password. If you forget it, use **Forgot password**. Nobody from the school will ever ask for your password.

## Your home page
**My children** shows each child (tabs if you have more than one): today's lessons, upcoming examinations, assignments due, recent marks, attendance this term, the latest report card, and school fees for this term. At the top you will see unread notifications and messages.

## What you can see
- **Assignments**: titles, deadlines and whether your child has handed in the work.
- **Timetable** and **Examinations**.
- **Attendance**: present, late and absent days, and you can send an **explanation** for an absence.
- **Results**: report cards once the school has published them, and **progress over time** (each term, and the yearly result, which is marked provisional until all three terms are published).
- **Quizzes** set for your child and the best score.

## School fees
Open **School fees**, then an invoice. You can **Pay with Paystack** (card, bank transfer or USSD) for the full amount or part of it. Nothing is counted as paid until Paystack confirms it; if you finished paying and it has not appeared, press **Check payment status**. You can also pay at the school office. Every payment has a receipt. Use **Download statement (PDF)** or the **PDF** button next to a payment to keep a copy. If the school has agreed an **instalment plan**, you will see the dates and amounts, and which are paid. You will get a reminder shortly before and after a due date.

## Messages
Under **Messages** you can write to the school office, or to a teacher who teaches your child, about one of your children. Teachers' phone numbers and emails are not shown. Please keep to school matters. Use **Report** on any message that worries you.

## My account
Update your **phone number** and **home address**. To change your name or email, contact the school office. If a link to a child is wrong (the relationship, a child to remove, or a child to add), use **Ask the school to correct a link**: the school checks it before changing anything, and you will be told the answer.

## Notification settings
Choose which messages you want in the app and by email: messages, fees, results, attendance, assignments, timetable changes and announcements. Account and security messages are always sent.

## Your privacy
You can only see your own children. The school keeps records as set out in its privacy notice. You can ask the school office for a copy of the information held about you.
