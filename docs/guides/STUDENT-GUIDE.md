# Guide for students

## Signing in
Your school gives you an activation link. Open it and choose a password only you know. Do not share it, not even with friends. If you forget it, use **Forgot password**.

## Your home page
**My class** shows today's lessons, upcoming examinations, assignments due, your latest marks, your attendance this term, and your newest report card. A bar at the top tells you if you have new notifications.

## Learning
- **My lessons**: notes, files and videos from your teachers.
- **Assignments**: see what is due and when. Open one, write your answer or attach your file, and press **Hand in**. You will see when it is marked, with your teacher's feedback. Hand in before the deadline: late work is shown as late.
- **Quizzes**: open a quiz, press **Start quiz**, and choose one answer for each question. Your answers are saved as you go and a clock shows the time left. When time ends, what you have answered is handed in for you. You see your score at once. Some quizzes also show the right answers afterwards. If your teacher allows more than one attempt, your best score counts.

## School life
- **Timetable** and **Examinations**: your lessons and exam dates.
- **Attendance**: your days present, late and absent.
- **My results**: report cards once the school publishes them, and **My progress** to see how each term compares.

## Notification settings
Choose which messages you want to receive. Account and security messages are always sent.

## Staying safe online
- Never give your password to anyone. Teachers and administrators will never ask for it.
- Sign out when you use a shared or school computer.
- Do not copy other students' work. Your teachers can see when work is handed in.
- If something on the site looks wrong or upsets you, tell a teacher or the school office.
